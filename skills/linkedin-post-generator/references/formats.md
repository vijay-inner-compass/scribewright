# Post formats

Templates for each format. Fill the brackets with material from the source notes. Keep the structure; adjust the wording to the user's voice.

Quick guide to which format suits which objective:

| Format | Branding | Thought leadership | Lead gen | Hiring | Launch | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Story | High | Medium | Medium | High | Medium | High |
| Lesson list | High | High | Medium | Low | Low | High |
| How-to | Medium | Medium | High | Low | Medium | Medium |
| Carousel outline | High | High | High | Medium | High | High |
| Poll | Low | Medium | Low | Low | Low | High |
| Short announcement | Low | Low | Medium | High | High | Low |
| Hot take | High | High | Low | Low | Low | High |

## Story post

Best for personal branding and engagement. Uses one real moment with a turn and a lesson.

```
[Hook: the moment, stated in one line]

[Context: where, when, what was at stake]

[What happened, in 2 to 4 short lines]

[The turn: what changed or what was learned]

[Takeaway in the user's voice]

[CTA, optional]
```

## Lesson list

Best for thought leadership and teaching. Three to five lessons, each with one line of explanation.

```
[Hook: "N things I learned from [context]" or a bold claim]

1. [Lesson] — [one-line why it matters]
2. [Lesson] — [one-line why it matters]
3. [Lesson] — [one-line why it matters]

[Closing line that ties them together]

[CTA]
```

Keep the list to five items or fewer. If the notes hold more, split them across posts.

## How-to post

Best for lead generation and authority. A short, usable process.

```
[Hook: the outcome the reader wants]

Here's how to [outcome] in [N] steps:

→ Step 1: [action]
→ Step 2: [action]
→ Step 3: [action]

[Common mistake to avoid, one line]

[CTA: offer the full guide, checklist, or call]
```

## Carousel outline

LinkedIn carousels are PDF documents with one slide per page. Write the slide text and a short caption. The user builds the PDF in Canva, PowerPoint, or similar.

```
Caption (post text):
[Hook line]
[One or two lines on what the carousel covers]
Swipe for [N] slides.
[CTA]

Slide 1 (cover): [Title, max 8 words]
Slide 2: [One point, max 25 words]
Slide 3: [One point, max 25 words]
...
Slide [N] (close): [Takeaway] + [CTA]
```

Aim for 6 to 10 slides. Each slide carries one point.

## Poll

LinkedIn polls take a question and up to four options. Keep the question short and the options parallel.

```
Question (max 140 characters): [question]
Option 1: [short option]
Option 2: [short option]
Option 3: [short option]
Option 4: [short option]

Post text (optional, adds context):
[One or two lines on why the question matters and what the user thinks]
```

## Short announcement

Best for launches, hiring, and milestones. Lead with the news, then the reason.

```
[News in the first line: what, and when if relevant]

[Why it matters, in 1 to 2 lines]

[Who it's for, or what's next]

[CTA: link, date, or how to get involved]
```

## Hot take

Best for thought leadership and engagement. A clear position, a reason, and an invitation to disagree.

```
[Position stated plainly, in one line]

[Why you think this, in 2 to 3 lines, using a specific example from the notes]

[The strongest counterpoint, acknowledged]

[Question that invites a response]
```

Use a hot take only when the notes contain a real opinion. Do not invent controversy.

## Choosing formats for a batch

- Mix at least three formats in a batch of five or more, so the feed does not feel repetitive.
- Put one story or personal post early in a series to build trust before the teaching posts.
- Put the CTA-heavy posts (lead gen, hiring, launch) after the audience has started to engage with the teaching posts.
- Keep a carousel or poll in the middle of the schedule for variety.
